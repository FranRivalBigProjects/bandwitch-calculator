How bandwidth estimation works

What counts as bandwidth in web hosting?

Bandwidth (or data transfer) is the total amount of data sent from your server to visitors' browsers in a month: HTML, CSS, JS, images, videos and API responses all count.

Does browser caching reduce bandwidth usage?

Yes. Repeat visitors often load cached assets from their own browser instead of downloading them again, which is why factoring in a cache ratio gives a more realistic estimate than raw page weight times views.

Why do hosting plans advertise "unlimited bandwidth"?

Most "unlimited" plans have fair-use policies. Knowing your real bandwidth need helps you spot a plan that will actually throttle or charge overages once you exceed a hidden threshold.
